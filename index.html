How to Draw in the Air

1
Index Finger Mode (Default) Raise your index finger up towards the camera to draw. Fold your other fingers into a fist. Fold index finger down to stop drawing.
2
Pinch Gesture Mode Pinch your index fingertip and thumb together to activate the pen. Separate them to move cursor without leaving marks.
3
AI Recognition Draw numbers (0-9) or simple shapes (Circle, Star, Heart, Square, Triangle, etc.) and click Recognize Air Drawing to analyze what you drew!
